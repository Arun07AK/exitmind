**Design results on Rinker Hall** (model results under stated assumptions; they say nothing about the real building)

- Bottlenecks, 200 runs in 11.9 s: the south stair's exterior door (EX-S) carries 22.9% of the flow with 9% of the exit width; the two east doors are under-used. Top queues: EX-S door, the lobby door of the central stair, the large classroom door.

- Crash-test rating, 24 scenarios per cell: college fire 10/24, college crush 4/24, college threat 4/24, everything else 0/24. School and mall put several times the printed occupant load in the building. After an earthquake, people who cannot use stairs have no way down in the model (no evacuation chairs, elevator out of use); adding chairs made 8 of 10 failing earthquake scenarios pass in a one-off check. Hospital cells fail on assisted patients waiting for the elevator.

**Deviations from the blueprint**

- Files are grouped differently from blueprint 4.4 (one `world.ts` instead of step/movement/portals/stairs; no `navgraph.ts`). Routing uses per-exit distance fields on the grid.
- Frames are JSON over WebSocket, not binary.
- Adversary budget is population 16 × 5 generations × 3 seeds (blueprint: 48 × 8 × 3) and the rating uses 24 scenarios per cell (blueprint: 50), to fit the time available; both are `runtime.json` values.
- Rating criterion: a scenario is survived when everyone is out or protected by the target, no doorway flow collapses, and there is no gridlock. The blueprint said "densityDangerPS is zero"; that failed every scenario because any pushing queue crosses the threshold for a moment. The density figure is still shown for each breaking scenario.
- Clearance targets are 600 s (fire, crush), 720 s (earthquake), 300 s (threat), raised from one 360 s default.
- `density.danger` 4 → 5 p/m², `crush.recoverDensity` 3.5 → 4.5 (both at the top of their ranges), smoke no longer makes cells impassable, new parameters `announce.premoveFactor`, `assist.elevatorTripSec`, `assist.handlingSec`, `premove.staffFactor`, `perception.signRadiusM`, `herding.radiusM`, `quake.aftershockWindowSec` — all labelled assumptions.
- Distilled policy keys options by action + most salient role (NEAREST, FAMILIAR, …) rather than the full role set; the first version reproduced almost nothing outside the exact logged option sets and stranded people.
- The Verifier's "worse than doing nothing" test is a paired comparison over 3 seeds and needs the difference to exceed 2 standard errors.
- Persona deadline is 6 s (blueprint 2.5 s); measured Haiku latency is about 1.5 s median per call.
- `nl2params` uses Haiku with a lexicon fallback; the Jev `score` path is not built.
- Historian updates the playbook entry with the observed outcome and adds breaking scenarios; it does not rebuild the distilled policy (it tells the operator to run `npm run distill`).
- Event types added: `demo.control`, `sign.state_sent`. Payload schemas exist only for `session.started`.
- Optimiser has no "add stair" edit and no candidate exit sites were traced, so "add exit" is never offered on Rinker.

**Bugs found by checking surprising results** (all fixed, all covered by the existing tests or the design re-runs)

- Float32 distance fields compared against float64 values left most of a room unreachable.
- Persona calls truncated at `max_tokens` (35–47% fell back).
- `Object.entries` order of zone counts made the Verifier's population differ between a live estimate and a replay.
- Sheltering people stopped in doorways; wheelchair users waiting at a stair blocked its entrance; two people heading opposite ways in a one-cell gap deadlocked (now they swap).
- Flow collapse at a door could never recover while any queue remained.
- Runs ended while people were still waiting for the elevator.
- A stale zone instruction was reused on the next floor, sending people back to a stair they had just left.
- Worker pool threads were unref'd, so CLIs exited mid-run.

**Known gaps**

- No behaviour or hazard parameter has been checked against its source.
- F1 outline is 4.9% under the printed GSF; F2 and F3 are within 2% before voids are deducted (`docs/verification/twin-spotcheck.md`).
- Code check: dead-end figures on F2 (26.2 ft) and F3 (39.5 ft) exceed 20 ft by an approximate grid measure; 94 interior doors use the assumed 34" leaf.
